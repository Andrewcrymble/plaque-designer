Outer-ring hanging loop

Loop ON by default. It appears on the front ring in both the assembled and 2-parts previews and is included in the ring SVG export.
The loop is physically integrated into the FRONT OUTER RING. The laser follows one continuous outer perimeter around the circle and loop, so the loop remains attached. The hanging hole is a separate internal cut.

Preview colours

These colours are visual only and do not alter the laser cut/engrave instructions.
SVG stays vector. PNG/JPG/WebP are embedded as raster images in the exported SVG.
Choose SVG, PNG, JPG/JPEG or WebP. Default: fill the complete 150 mm back plate and crop at the circular edge.
Default artwork frame: exact 135 mm circle. The artwork centre is mathematically locked to the same centre point as the inner red cut circle.

Laser image cleanup

Pixels lighter than the threshold become transparent. Dark notes/lines remain. Increase the threshold to keep more grey detail; reduce it to keep only the darkest marks.
Cleanup is always applied to the exported Falcon-ready back plate when enabled.
Centred means X = 0 and Y = 0 at the exact centre of the circular plaque.
By default the artwork is scaled, centred and clipped to the exact 135 mm inner circle. Its centre point matches the plaque centre. X/Y controls are only for deliberate fine adjustment.

Laser operation settings

These settings are embedded as layer/group names and SVG metadata. Falcon Design Space may still require you to assign the values after import; SVG itself cannot universally force machine speed/power.

Laser-ready side-by-side export

Defaults: Main wording 40, small wording 40, verse Courier 10 at Y 133. Verse is red in the design preview; in the laser export it is automatically converted to BLACK because BLACK = ENGRAVE.
LEFT PART — BACK PLATE
• 150 mm outside circle = CUT
• Reference / verse = ENGRAVE
• Background image = ENGRAVE

RIGHT PART — FRONT RING + WORDING
• Outside circle + integrated hanging tab = CUT
• Hole in hanging tab = CUT
• 135 mm inside circle = CUT
• Main wording = CUT
• Small wording = CUT

Both physical parts are positioned side by side in the same SVG. The downloaded filename is automatically taken from the Main wording.

Laser colour rule: RED = CUT   |   BLACK = ENGRAVE.
Default operation settings: Cut 350 mm/min @ 100%; Image engrave 8000 mm/min @ 80%; Line/reference engrave 3500 mm/min @ 40%.
